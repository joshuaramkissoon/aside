A Look Up replacement for macOS

What it means here.

Point at a word in any app and press ⌥ Space. Aside reads the sentence around it and explains it on the spot, in about a third of a second. Then you can keep asking.

Download for Mac

macOS 15.2 or later. Free and open source.

The difference

Look Up reads one word. Aside reads the sentence.

The details

Small things it gets right.

1No selection needed

Point at any part of a term and Aside works out the whole of it. Point at “ahead” and it explains the write-ahead log.

On restart, Postgres replays the write-ahead log from the last checkpoint.

write-ahead logRecovery

Changes are logged before they touch the data files, so a crash can be replayed from it.

2Acronyms spelled out

The expansion comes first, with the initials picked out, so the acronym reads itself.

Evict with LRU once the cache passes 512 MB.

LRUCache policy

Least Recently Used

Discards the entry untouched longest when the cache is full.

3Keep asking

The input is already focused, so just type. ⌘1 and ⌘2 ask the suggested follow-ups.

flushI/O operation

Hands buffered writes to the OS, but does not guarantee they reach disk.
