Four steps: write vs flush vs fsync vs fsyncing the directory.

Notes write vs flush vs fsync vs fsyncing the directory0.34s
flushI/O operation

Hands buffered writes to the OS, but does not guarantee they reach disk. That’s fsync’s job.

  • flush moves bytes from your buffer to the kernel’s page cache; fsync forces them onto the disk.
  • Survives a process crash, not a power cut.
